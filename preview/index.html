We help experienced professionals move into the roles in AI safety where they'll matter most. We do this by routing individuals to the roles that fit them, by building hiring intelligence so organisations can hire well from outside the field, and by coordinating the talent infrastructure around them.

Routing individuals

For people with years of experience in another field, and for people already in AI safety who want a role that fits them better.

  1. 1Talk

    A 30-minute call about what you've done so far and what you want to do next.

  2. 2Assess

    We tell you where we think you'd fit, including roles that aren't advertised.

  3. 3Refer

    We send you roles to look at, and refer you where we think you'd be a good match.

How it works for talent

Hiring intelligence

For organisations hiring experienced people, especially from outside the field.

  • Scouting

    Send us a role, including closed rounds, and we'll suggest people from the 250 or so we've spoken to.

  • Calibration

    We help you read CVs from consulting, law or government, where the signals look different.

  • Better processes

    We're researching how other fields hire senior people, and which parts of AI safety hiring put them off.

How we work with organisations

Talent infrastructure

For people partway through a move, and the organisations supporting them.

  • Programmes

    We help out with programmes like Lateral and GenStream, and have a grant pot to incubate new projects.

  • Events

    We run events for experienced professionals, such as an evening at EAG New York with GenStream and Lateral.

  • Coordination

    We swap hard-to-fill roles with SteadRise, Successif and others.

More on talent infrastructure

So far

Over the past few months we have spoken to about 250 experienced professionals, made more than 200 referrals and tracked hundreds of roles. About 40 organisations have worked with us on hiring, mostly on closed, senior rounds.

People we have worked with are now at: BlueDot Impact, MATS, Anthropic, Coefficient Giving, GovAI, IAPS and more.
