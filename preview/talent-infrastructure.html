Programmes we have helped

Short, intensive programmes for experienced professionals exploring AI safety.

Lateral

A weekend workshop for experienced professionals from other fields who are exploring a move into AI safety. One-to-ones, small group discussions, talks from people in the field and a mixer, followed by virtual check-ins.

Where
Berkeley
Length

GenStream

A fully funded week for people becoming AI safety generalists, in roles like operations, programme management, talent, communications and field-building. Each cohort of 8 to 12 takes on a real problem from an AI safety organisation and builds a working project in five days.

Where
London, at LISA
Length
One week
Run by
Yaqoob Ahmad and Julien Sireau
About GenStream
