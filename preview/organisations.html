Scouting

Most of the roles we work on are closed rounds or were never advertised.

  1. 1Tell us

    Send us the role, or describe the kind of person you'd hire if you met them.

  2. 2We look

    We look through the experienced professionals we've spoken to, more than 110 so far.

  3. 3We refer

    We send a few people we'd back for the role, each with a short note on why.

If you have strong runners-up you can't hire, let us know. With their agreement, we'll refer them to other roles.

Questions

How many candidates will you send?
Usually a few. We only have time to look closely at a handful of people per role, so we send the ones we think fit best.
Do you charge?
No.
Can you help if we have no open role?
Yes. We can talk through a role you're thinking about opening, or the kind of person you'd like to meet.
How do you handle candidates' details?
We only share someone's details with you once they've agreed.
