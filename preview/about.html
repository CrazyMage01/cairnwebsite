Lots of experienced people want to work on AI safety, and it's hard to find a way in.

Why we exist

AI safety has lots of excellent young organisations, and many of them need people who've run teams and scaled organisations before.

There are strong pipelines into the field for students, but far fewer for experienced professionals outside those networks. Hiring managers are often unsure how to read their CVs, so good candidates get overlooked and senior searches drag on.

How it started

AI Safety Routing grew out of a project Sam ran at the Generator Residency at Constellation in Berkeley in 2026. One form shared in a couple of Slack channels drew over 100 responses. A few months and a couple of hundred conversations later, it was clear there was enough here for an organisation.

How we work

We send a few referrals rather than many. We only share someone's details with their permission. And we share open roles with other talent organisations, so people get a clearer picture of what's out there.
